South Sacramento — languages spoken at home

Research and synthesis by Bao Thien Ngo / cPALSs. “South Sacramento” is not a Census place. These tables use ACS 2020–2024 5-year C16001 estimates for corridor proxies: Florin CDP, Parkway CDP, and ZCTAs 95823, 95824, and 95828 (Little Saigon / Stockton Blvd belt).

Compared with Elk Grove city, these South Sacramento proxies are far less English-only (often mid-40s%) and far more Spanish. Vietnamese is especially concentrated in Florin and ZCTA 95828. The ACS “Other Asian and Pacific Island languages” bucket is large here; in Sacramento County that bucket is driven heavily by Hmong (also named in the County’s elections language-access materials).

Festival guests travel: Elk Grove Park draws from both the venue city and this corridor. Partner translation for regional outreach should not use Elk Grove city alone.

How cPALSs uses this for festival partners

Priority for translated partner materials: Vietnamese, English, and Spanish — same priority set as Elk Grove, reinforced here by Spanish dominance and Vietnamese concentration in Florin / 95828.

Nice to have: Hmong (via the large Other Asian & Pacific Island share) and Chinese. Hmong is a stronger South Sacramento nice-to-have than inside Elk Grove city.

Honest capacity note: cPALSs is not currently marketing in Chinese or Hmong as standing campaign languages. We are still looking for a director of marketing to help build a multilingual team. If a partner can produce Chinese or Hmong materials, that helps corridor reach; it is not paired with a promise that cPALSs already runs matching multilingual paid or organic campaigns in those languages.
