Vietnamese American demographics › Population & geography

Population & geography

Research and synthesis by Bao Thien Ngo.

Sacramento County is the core local public for workshop and partner briefings: 35,146 Vietnamese people in ACS 2023 1-year PUMS — Vietnamese alone or in combination (RAC3P: Vietnamese alone; White/Vietnamese; Chinese/Vietnamese; Vietnamese/Other Asian), per Bao’s county synthesis. Surrounding counties below use the same definition and vintage.

Inside the county, about 38.6% live in Elk Grove and 35.2% in Sacramento proper (Bao’s PUMA grouping). A large share of the remainder is in unincorporated areas — including much of the Little Saigon corridor on Stockton Boulevard — that city-only maps undercount. For many Elk Grove households the boulevard is a destination, not home.
