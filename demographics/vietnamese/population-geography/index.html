Population & geography

Research and synthesis by Bao Thien Ngo.

Sacramento County and the metro

Sacramento County is the core local public used in workshop and partner briefings: about 35,146 Vietnamese people in ACS 2023, per Bao’s Census data on Vietnamese in Sacramento County. That scale is small enough that a six-figure festival audience cannot be “the community showing up” alone — it requires neighbors who are not Vietnamese.

Greater Sacramento in Census terms is the Sacramento–Roseville–Folsom metro (Sacramento, Placer, Yolo, and El Dorado Counties). ACS 2024 5-year, table B02015 Vietnamese alone, puts the MSA at about 37,866 (±2,565). Adding San Joaquin County (Stockton drive market, not in the MSA) adds about 8,217 — roughly ~46k if the question is catchment, not a single county.

County Vietnamese-alone 5-year counts and Bao’s 2023 county synthesis are the same place counted two ways. Use ~35k when partners already know that figure; use metro / Stockton-inclusive numbers when the question is drive-market reach.

Where people live inside the county

Bao’s ACS 2023 county synthesis groups geography so that about 38.6% of county Vietnamese live in Elk Grove, 35.2% in the City of Sacramento, and the rest elsewhere in the county — roughly two-thirds in those two named cities.

Place-level Vietnamese-alone counts (ACS 2024 5-year) put Elk Grove first among incorporated cities (~9,800), Sacramento city second (~8,200), with Roseville, Rancho Cordova, Davis, Folsom, and others thinner. A large share of county Vietnamese live in unincorporated South Sacramento / Fruitridge / Laguna-adjacent tracts — including much of the Little Saigon corridor on Stockton Boulevard — that a city-only map undercounts.

Organizer implication: a Facebook group titled “Sacramento” does not mean the poster lives here, and an Elk Grove flyer does not automatically reach unincorporated corridor elders unless someone forwards it. Little Saigon is often a destination, not home for households that raised kids on the boulevard and now live in Elk Grove.

National scale

Nationally, about 2.3 million people identified as Vietnamese in the U.S. in recent ACS cuts; 44% were U.S.-born in the 2022 cut summarized in Vietnamese American demographics (2012 vs 2022). Use national scale for proportion and trend — not as a substitute for the Sacramento headcount.
