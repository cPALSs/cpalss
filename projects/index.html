Projects

Recurring & Ongoing Projects

Miss & Miss Teen Vietnam of Northern California ↗

Miss & Miss Teen Vietnam Northern California empowers young Vietnamese American women to achieve their goals and make a difference in the community.

Lunar New Year Tết ↗

Lunar New Year Tết celebrates Vietnamese and Asian heritage by preserving traditions, fostering family bonds, instilling cultural pride in younger generations. It also promotes cross-cultural unity in the region’s diverse community, offering residents a chance to experience and appreciate different traditions.

Installation Gala

The annual Installation Gala is a banquet that honors the accomplishments of community members.

Great Lantern Festival ↗

The Mid-Autumn Festival is a time for families to gather, appreciate the full moon, and give thanks for the harvest. The festival is branded as the Great Lantern Festival.

Elk Grove Toy Drive Celebration ↗

The annual Elk Grove Toy Drive Celebration engages the community in holiday celebrations while collecting toys to donate to local charities supporting children in need.

Phở for Seniors

Sharing phở from local small businesses with underserved seniors. Started during the COVID-19 pandemic, and coming back. Sign-ups are open.
